Guava alpha privacy notice

Updated October 18, 2026. Guava is an experimental driving map and road game for a small group of testers. Do not rely on it as your only navigation or traffic source.

Your account

Google sign-in provides Guava with a basic account identifier, email address, and name. Supabase stores your Guava account and display name. Other players do not see your email address. Your community profile is off by default.

Location and drives

The app requests location while the web page is open. It records a drive after you tap Start. Full GPS samples for saved drives are stored in this browser. They may be lost if you clear browser storage or switch devices. You can export them in Settings.

If you explicitly join community play under the current notice, Guava sends short GPS segments over HTTPS to its Supabase function while you drive after you have traveled at least 50 m. The function sends sampled coordinates to the public OSRM road-matching service. Guava stores matched road-unit visits with their day, your account identifier, and the session’s timing—not the full raw GPS trace. Those visits can count even if you later discard the trip record. When you Finish & Save, the completed drive is sent for shared XP, distance and height. Guava stores the drive summary and scores, not the full GPS trace, in Supabase. Territory ownership becomes final the following morning, around 09:05 Israel time, to allow late uploads. Captured roads and your display name can reveal places you visit, including near home or work. Only enable community play if you are comfortable with this.

Turning community play off removes your shared drives, scores, and claims from Guava’s database. Your browser’s saved drives remain until you clear site data. Previously viewed or copied information cannot be recalled from other people.

Other services

Google handles sign-in; Supabase hosts account and game data. The map and route experience also requests data from OpenFreeMap/OpenStreetMap, Photon search, OSRM routing and matching, elevation tiles, and Open-Meteo elevation. These services may receive network information and the coordinates needed for the feature you use. Guava does not sell your data.

Alpha limitations and contact

Browser location may stop when the screen locks or the tab is in the background. Road matching, traffic, scores and navigation can be incomplete or wrong. This notice may change as the alpha evolves. For privacy questions, use the support contact shown on Guava’s Google sign-in screen.

Drive safely. Never interact with Guava’s game controls while moving. Follow road signs and use a trusted navigation app for critical directions.
