Guava

Every road is a little adventure.

Guava is a mobile-friendly driving map and experimental road game for friends. Search for a destination or start a free drive, explore a normal or altitude-colored map, and record your distance, time and height traveled.

Google sign-in gives each tester a Guava account. With community play off, drives stay private in that browser. If you choose to join community play, newly completed drives can earn shared XP and appear on leaderboards. Each day, the driver with the most separate drives across a road section can capture it; a tie leaves it uncolored.

Before you drive

Guava is an alpha, not a replacement for a trusted navigation app. It does not have live traffic, reliable background navigation or safety-grade turn guidance. Use it for testing and exploration, and do not operate game controls while moving.
